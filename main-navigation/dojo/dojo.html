What is Dojo?

Dojo is a startup I founded in 2023. It is a social platform designed for creative communities to create, share, collaborate, and grow together. I realized that college clubs centered around photography, graphic design, and other creative mediums did not have a place that "spoke their language".

These art clubs don't need text threads that don't allow multiple conversations at once, if three people send things they've made, replies to individual creations suddenly congeal into something impossible to understand.
