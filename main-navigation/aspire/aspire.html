What is Aspire?

Aspire was a startup i founded in Early 2025, it was designed to be a social productivity app. Someone would log on and create a block scheduling system for themself. Then, theyd put in goals and things they want to acomplish, and the app would help them split that goal up into pieces. It would then connect them to groups with similar goals and interest to hold each other accountable. Users could earn points by completing the sub goals of their larger goals and help make the experience more fun. Ultimately it was shuttered when it was realized the market timing for dojo was just too good to pass up.
